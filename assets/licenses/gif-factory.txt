GIF Factory algorithms and workflow reference
Upstream: https://github.com/WoYun520/gif-factory
Reviewed revision: 17fcba20f19e8b3e64c448a574de384d767b23ab

Xuzuo Studio adapts the atlas/light-motion, keyframe, shared-palette, GIF
verification, and two-state lenticular composition methods. The desktop tool
is an offline adapter; it does not include upstream character examples,
third-party photographs, music, or brand-film assets.
